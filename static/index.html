ResQ Golden Rules of First Aid
1. Check for danger — ensure the scene is safe before helping anyone
2. Call 112 — immediately for any serious emergency, do not delay
3. Don't move casualties with suspected spinal or neck injuries
4. Stay calm — reassure, speak clearly and confidently
5. Recovery position — unconscious but breathing? Place on their side
